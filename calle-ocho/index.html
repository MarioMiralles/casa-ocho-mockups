On foot

  1. 1

    ~15 min walk

    The heart of Little Havana: ventanitas pouring cafecito, cigar shops, fruit stands, galleries and murals.

  2. 2

    ~15 to 20 min

    Better known as Domino Park. Locals play dominoes here every day; stop and watch a game.

  3. 3
    Tower Theater
    ~15 to 20 min

    A historic Art Deco movie house right on Calle Ocho, beside the park.

  4. 4
    Live music on the strip
    ~15 to 20 min

    Salsa, son and Latin jazz drift out of the bars and patios along Calle Ocho in the evenings, especially on weekends.
