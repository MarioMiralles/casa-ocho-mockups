04The Neighborhood

Little Havana on foot, Miami in ten minutes.

Little Havana is Miami's Cuban heart: ventanitas pouring cafecito, domino games in Máximo Gómez Park, cigar shops and live music along Calle Ocho. The house sits on a quiet street about fifteen minutes away on foot, close enough to walk to breakfast and far enough to sleep well.
